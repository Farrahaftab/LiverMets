**Manuscript:** Prognostic survival prediction and risk stratification in colorectal liver metastases (Paper 1). The locked v8 analysis is unchanged. Full detail: *Paper1_supervisor_confirmation_note.docx*.

**Major scientific issues**

1. **Predictor timing (gating).** Were metastasis count and size recorded before any preoperative treatment? Unconfirmed; no data dictionary available. Registry confirmation needed.
2. **33.8% excluded for incomplete TNM staging** (8,832 of 26,094). Excluded patients differ; missing-at-random cannot be verified. Direction of selection bias unknown.
3. **Follow-up limits.** Validation median follow-up 1.47 years; only 26% evaluable at 5 years; no 5-year calibration for 2016+ operations.
4. **Calibration.** The model under-predicts 5-year survival (40.8% vs 46.6%) and has not been recalibrated. Discrimination is modest (C-index 0.622). No external validation.
5. **Data quality.** 16 probable duplicate pairs; 3,074 deceased patients without a death flag; 1,676 recurrences without a usable date.
6. **M2a confidence-interval discrepancy.** Upper bound 0.637 (candidate table) vs 0.636 (selected-model output). The manuscript uses 0.636. Supervisor to confirm.
7. **Analysis choices.** Headline horizon (5 vs 3 years); whether clinician thresholds are added; whether a protocol predates validation.
