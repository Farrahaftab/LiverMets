Statistical Analyses: Kaplan–Meier | Log-rank tests | Cox regression | Propensity score matching | Temporal validation
KEY FINDINGS
  • CART-derived phenotypes significantly stratified OS and RFS (χ²(2)=224.89, p<0.001, n=14,759)
  • Favourable phenotype (26.8%): median OS 6.49 years | Intermediate (18.1%): 5.55 years | Adverse (55.2%): 4.06 years
  • Multivariable Cox: Adverse phenotype HR = 1.616 (p<0.001); Surgery+Chemo HR = 0.931 (p=0.031); C-index = 0.603
  • Propensity score matching: No OS benefit of surgery+chemo over surgery alone in favourable and intermediate phenotypes; exploratory signal in adverse phenotype (p=0.045)
  • Temporal validation confirmed phenotype prognostic value in both earlier (≤2009) and later (>2009) cohorts (p<0.001 both eras)
TNM: Tumor-Node-Metastasis | OS: Overall Survival | RFS: Recurrence-Free Survival | CART: Classification and Regression Trees | SO: Surgery Only | SC: Surgery+Chemo | Tx: Treatment | NS: Not Significant | SE: Survival estimate | PSM: Propensity Score Matching | p: p-value
